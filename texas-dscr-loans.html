Texas DSCR Loans

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 25, 2026

What are ACP’s Texas DSCR loan terms?

A DSCR loan qualifies a rental in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin or anywhere else in Texas on its rent instead of your income, so no tax returns or pay stubs. ACP Lending makes 30-year fixed DSCR loans from $50,000 at up to 80% of value on a purchase or rate-and-term refinance and 75% on cash-out, with no minimum DSCR on some programs and no minimum credit score on one. Rates start around 7.75% with 1 point for good credit. Loans close in an LLC or your own name, in Texas and most other states.

DSCR loans in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio and Austin

The terms above apply in all four metros. Some programs exclude rural property at the edges, so send the address first.

Houston

ACP is based in Spring, north of Houston, and makes DSCR rental loans across Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery, Brazoria and Galveston counties, including Katy, Sugar Land, Pearland, The Woodlands and Conroe. If the property is in a FEMA flood zone, most lenders require flood insurance, so price it before you make an offer.

Dallas–Fort Worth

ACP makes DSCR rental loans across Dallas, Tarrant, Collin and Denton counties, including Dallas, Fort Worth, Plano, Frisco, McKinney and Arlington.

San Antonio

ACP makes DSCR rental loans across Bexar County and nearby, including New Braunfels, Schertz and Boerne.

Austin

ACP makes DSCR rental loans across Travis, Williamson and Hays counties, including Round Rock, Georgetown, Pflugerville, Cedar Park and San Marcos.

DSCR loans outside Texas

ACP also makes these loans in most other states, including Oklahoma, Illinois, Michigan, Indiana, Georgia and Virginia, on the same programs. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property, so send the address and ACP will match it to a program that lends there.

Outside Texas, lower property taxes often make it easier to reach a 1.00 coverage ratio, which can mean better pricing on the same rent.

Texas DSCR loan questions

Do I need tax returns or pay stubs for a Texas DSCR loan?

No. A DSCR loan qualifies on the property’s rent. You provide the lease or a market-rent estimate, bank statements for reserves and your entity documents.

Why do Texas rentals have trouble hitting a 1.00 DSCR?

Property taxes and insurance. Texas has no state income tax, so property taxes run high, and insurance near the coast has climbed. A $300,000 house renting for $2,200 with a $2,500 full payment comes out at 0.88. That’s why programs with no minimum DSCR matter in Texas.

What if my DSCR is under 1.00?

Some programs have no minimum DSCR and others go as low as 0.75x. Expect a slightly higher rate or lower leverage. If the rent still won’t carry it, a conventional or bank-statement rental loan may fit better.

Can I do a cash-out refinance right after I finish a rehab?

Yes. There’s no 12-month wait. The loan is sized on the finished appraised value, up to 75% on cash-out.

Does the property need a tenant?

Not on 1–4 units. Vacant properties qualify on market rent.

Can I use a DSCR loan for an Airbnb or short-term rental in Texas?

Yes, on some programs, qualified on short-term rental income.

Do you make DSCR loans outside Texas?

Yes. ACP makes these loans in most other states, including Oklahoma, Illinois, Michigan, Indiana, Georgia and Virginia, on the same programs. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property, so send the address and ACP will match it to a program that lends there.

Can I close in my LLC?

Yes. DSCR loans close in an LLC or your personal name.

What credit score do I need?

One program has no minimum credit score, with leverage stepping down below 675. Most programs price best at 680 and up.

My hard money loan has matured. Can I refinance into a DSCR loan?

Yes. If you’ve kept making the monthly payments, a matured loan is treated as current for the refinance.

How do I get a quote?

Send the property address, purchase price or value, the rent, taxes, insurance and HOA, and your middle credit score. ACP compares the deal across 90+ loan programs and comes back, usually the same day, with the loan amount, cash to close and coverage ratio.

Bridge & Fix & Flip Loans

Short-term financing for the purchase and renovation of an investment property, repaid when it sells or refinances.

Terms

  • Up to 100% of purchase and 100% of renovation (tiered by credit and experience)
  • Rate: 9% to 12%, interest-only
  • Points: 1 to 2
  • Term: 12 to 24 months
  • No prepayment penalty
  • Closing in 5 business days on a complete file

The client pays all closing costs of the lender and third parties. Subject to underwriting.

Bridge Loans for Investors: Texas and Nationwide

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 25, 2026

What is a bridge loan for a real estate investor?

A short-term, interest-only loan that carries an investment property until it is sold or refinanced into long-term debt. ACP Lending places bridge loans in Texas and most other states, from $75,000, for purchases, renovations, and refinances of hard money loans that have matured or are about to. Terms run 6 to 24 months, rates start at 9.99%, and the loan is sized on the property’s value and your exit.

Bridge loans in Texas

ACP Lending is based in Spring, Texas, north of Houston, and places bridge loans statewide, including Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and their suburbs.

Many Texas investors come to ACP with a hard money loan that has matured or is about to, on a house that isn’t finished or sold. If you’ve kept the monthly payments current, the matured loan is treated as current, and it can be refinanced into a new bridge loan to finish the work or a 30-year DSCR loan if the house can rent now.

Loan coming due and the property isn’t sold or finished? Send the payoff letter and the current status. A matured loan with payments kept current is treated as current.

Bridge loan questions

My hard money loan is maturing and the house isn’t done. What can I do?

Refinance into a new bridge loan to finish the work, or into a 30-year DSCR loan if the property is rentable now. Send the payoff letter, the remaining scope of work and the current value. If you’ve kept making the monthly payments, a matured loan is treated as current.

Can I refinance out of a bridge loan into a long-term rental loan?

Yes. Once the property is finished, a 30-year DSCR loan can pay off the bridge, sized on the finished appraised value. No 12-month wait, including cash-out, and no tenant needed on 1–4 units.

How much can I borrow on a bridge refinance?

Generally up to 70% of the current value on a refinance, plus any remaining renovation budget held back for draws, depending on credit, experience and the program.

Which states do you make bridge loans in?

Texas, where ACP is based, and most other states. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property.

Do you make bridge loans in Houston and Dallas?

Yes. ACP places bridge loans statewide in Texas, including Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and their suburbs.

Is a bridge loan the same as a hard money loan?

Close to it. Both are short-term, interest-only loans sized mainly on the property. “Hard money” usually means a private lender with higher rates; bridge programs from institutional lenders often cost less.

How do I get a quote?

Send the property address, what you owe and when it matures (or the purchase price), the repairs left, the value, and your middle credit score. ACP compares the deal across 90+ loan programs and comes back, usually the same day.

Referral Partner Program

Know a real estate investor who needs financing? Refer them to ACP. We analyze the deal, structure the terms and close the loan, and you earn a referral fee on every loan we fund, paid at closing.

How it works

  1. You refer. Sign up below, then send us the investor's name and deal by call, text or email.
  2. We fund. We underwrite and close the loan. You stay the trusted contact.
  3. You get paid. Your referral fee is paid at closing on every funded loan. No funding, no cost to you.

Who partners with us

  • Realtors working with investor buyers
  • Wholesalers with buyers who need funding
  • CPAs and bookkeepers with investor clients
  • Title and escrow officers
  • Contractors, property managers, insurance agents and appraisers

What we finance

Fix & flip and bridge, DSCR rental purchase and cash-out refinance, ground-up construction, 2–4 units, multifamily, portfolios, and commercial and mixed-use property. Business-purpose loans only.

Become a partner

Referral fees are paid only on funded loans under a signed referral agreement. Privacy Policy.

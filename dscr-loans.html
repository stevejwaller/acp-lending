DSCR Loans: Texas and Nationwide

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 25, 2026

What are ACP’s DSCR loan terms?

A DSCR loan qualifies a rental on its rent instead of your income, so no tax returns or pay stubs. ACP Lending places 30-year fixed DSCR loans in Texas and most other states, from $75,000, at up to 80% of value on a purchase or rate-and-term refinance and 75% on cash-out, with no minimum DSCR on some programs and no minimum credit score on one. Rates start around 7.75% with 1 point for good credit. Loans close in an LLC or your own name.

DSCR loans in Texas

ACP Lending is based in Spring, Texas, north of Houston, and places DSCR loans statewide, including Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and their suburbs.

Texas rentals are harder to qualify on rent than rentals in many other states. Texas has no state income tax, so property taxes run high, and insurance near the coast has climbed. A $300,000 house renting for $2,200 with a $2,500 full payment, taxes and insurance included, comes out at 0.88.

That’s why the programs with no minimum DSCR, or a 0.75x minimum, matter most in Texas. Run your own rent, taxes and insurance through the DSCR calculator.

DSCR loan questions

Do I need tax returns or pay stubs for a DSCR loan?

No. A DSCR loan qualifies on the property’s rent. You provide the lease or a market-rent estimate, bank statements for reserves and your entity documents.

Which states do you make DSCR loans in?

Texas, where ACP is based, and most other states. That includes Oklahoma, Illinois, Michigan, Indiana, Georgia and Virginia. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property.

Why do Texas rentals have trouble hitting a 1.00 DSCR?

Property taxes and insurance. Texas has no state income tax, so property taxes run high, and insurance near the coast has climbed. A $300,000 house renting for $2,200 with a $2,500 full payment comes out at 0.88. That’s why programs with no minimum DSCR matter in Texas. See DSCR loans in Texas.

What if my DSCR is under 1.00?

Some programs have no minimum DSCR and others go as low as 0.75x. Expect a slightly higher rate or lower leverage. If the rent still won’t carry it, a conventional or bank-statement rental loan may fit better.

Can I do a cash-out refinance right after I finish a rehab?

Yes. There’s no 12-month wait. The loan is sized on the finished appraised value, up to 75% on cash-out.

Does the property need a tenant?

Not on 1–4 units. Vacant properties qualify on market rent.

Can I use a DSCR loan for an Airbnb or short-term rental?

Yes, on some programs, qualified on short-term rental income.

Can I close in my LLC?

Yes. DSCR loans close in an LLC or your personal name.

What credit score do I need?

One program has no minimum credit score, with leverage stepping down below 675. Most programs price best at 680 and up.

My hard money loan has matured. Can I refinance into a DSCR loan?

Yes. If you’ve kept making the monthly payments, a matured loan is treated as current for the refinance.

How do I get a quote?

Send the property address, purchase price or value, the rent, taxes, insurance and HOA, and your middle credit score. ACP compares the deal across 90+ loan programs and comes back, usually the same day, with the loan amount, cash to close and coverage ratio.

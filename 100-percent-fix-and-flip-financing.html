100% Fix and Flip Financing

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 25, 2026

What is 100% fix and flip financing?

A fix and flip loan (a type of hard money loan) that funds the full purchase price at closing and the full renovation budget in draws, so you bring no down payment. ACP Lending offers 100% programs for loans up to $1,250,000 in Texas and most other states, starting at a 650 credit score, as long as the total loan fits under 65% to 75% of the after-repair value. You still pay the loan’s closing costs: points, lender fees, prepaid interest, title and insurance.

We make 100% loans to borrowers with strong credit, experience, liquidity and collateral. You pay the lender costs (origination, processing and closing fees) and the third-party costs (title, insurance, legal and appraisal) at closing.

100% financing questions

What does 100% fix and flip financing cover?

The purchase price and the renovation budget. The purchase is funded at closing; the renovation money is held back and paid out in draws as the work is inspected.

What do I still pay with 100% financing?

The loan’s own costs: points, lender fees and prepaid interest, plus the normal purchase costs of title, appraisal, inspection, insurance and prorated property taxes. A seller credit can cover part of the lender costs. In this example, the investor’s cash at closing was $17,418 on an $889,000 loan.

Who qualifies for 100% financing on a flip?

Start at a 650 credit score. The higher your score and the more completed flips you have, the higher the after-repair value cap, which is what decides whether the whole project fits. With a 720+ score and 10 completed flips, a second program reaches 75% of ARV on loans up to $800,000.

Why does the after-repair value matter so much?

Because the loan can’t exceed the ARV cap. If purchase plus repairs is $889,000 and the cap is 75%, the appraisal has to come in at about $1,185,000 or higher for 100% to hold. If it comes in lower, you bring the difference. Check your ARV against closed sales in the subdivision before you sign the contract.

Is 100% financing available in Texas?

Yes, statewide. See Texas fix and flip loans for the full Texas terms.

Is 100% financing available outside Texas?

Yes, in most states. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property. Send the address and ACP will tell you which 100% program lends there.

Is 100% financing more expensive?

Usually yes, in points. The example deal paid 3 points. On a 90% program you pay less in points but bring 10% of the purchase price, which is often $50,000 to $100,000 more cash at the table.

How do I find out if my deal fits?

Send the property address, purchase price, repair budget, after-repair value, your middle credit score and how many flips you have finished in the last three years. ACP compares the deal across 90+ loan programs and comes back, usually the same day, with the advance, the cash to close and the program that fits.

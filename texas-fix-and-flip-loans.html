Texas Hard Money Fix and Flip Loans

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 25, 2026

What are ACP’s Texas fix and flip loan terms?

ACP Lending makes fix and flip loans, often called hard money loans, on investment properties in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and the rest of Texas at up to 100% of the purchase price and 100% of the renovation budget, from $50,000 to $5,000,000. The loan is capped at 65% to 80% of after-repair value depending on the program, your credit and your experience. Rates start at 9.99%, terms run 6 to 24 months interest-only, and most programs take a 640 credit score or higher. First flips are considered, and ACP makes the same loans in most other states.

Texas fix and flip loan questions

Can I get 100% financing on a fix and flip in Texas?

Yes. ACP offers programs that fund 100% of the purchase and 100% of the renovation on Texas flips. Two things decide it. The total loan has to fit under the program’s after-repair value cap, which is 65% to 75% of ARV on these programs. And your credit and experience set that cap: a 650 score gets 65%, 700+ gets 70%, and 700+ with three completed flips gets 75%. A separate program goes to 75% of ARV for investors with a 720+ score and 10 completed flips, five of them in Texas, on loans up to $800,000.

100% financing covers the purchase and the repairs. You still pay points, lender fees, prepaid interest, title and insurance at closing. See what 100% financing covers and what you still pay.

How much cash do I need to close a Texas fix and flip loan?

On a 100% program, only the loan’s closing costs: points, lender fees, prepaid interest, title, appraisal and insurance. On a 90% to 95% program, add the 5% to 10% of the purchase price the loan doesn’t cover. In this north Texas example, an $889,000 loan closed with about $17,400 from the investor; on a typical 90% program the same deal needed roughly $90,000 to $100,000.

Lenders also want to see liquid funds after closing, usually six months of payments plus one-third of the renovation budget. You show it, you don’t spend it.

What credit score do I need for a fix and flip loan in Texas?

Some programs go down to 600, most start at 640 to 650, and the highest-leverage program for experienced investors needs 720. A higher score raises the after-repair value cap and lowers the rate.

Can I get a fix and flip loan in Texas with no experience?

Yes. Several programs take first-time flippers. Expect a lower after-repair value cap and a bit more cash at closing until you have completed flips on record. Heavy rehabs, where repairs cost more than the purchase price, usually need prior experience.

Is ACP a hard money lender?

Yes. ACP Lending lends to real estate investors. We finance hard money fix and flip loans in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and the rest of Texas, with 90+ loan programs, so one application is matched to the program that fits your deal. The fix and flip loans on this page are what most investors mean by hard money.

How is the loan amount calculated?

The loan is the lower of two tests: a percentage of total cost (purchase plus repairs) and a percentage of after-repair value. ACP builds the after-repair value from closed sales in the property’s own subdivision, the same way the appraiser will, so the number you plan on is the number the loan is sized on.

What rate and points should I expect?

Rates start at 9.99% for strong credit and experience, with 1% to 3% in points. The rate goes up and the leverage goes down as credit and experience go down. Current starting rates by credit score are on the rate page.

How fast can a Texas fix and flip loan close?

Quotes usually come back the same day. Closing typically takes 5 to 10 business days after a complete file is received, depending on the appraisal or valuation, title and insurance. Some programs skip a full appraisal, which saves time.

Do you lend in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio and Austin?

Yes. ACP makes Texas fix and flip loans statewide, including Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and their suburbs. Some programs exclude rural property.

Do you make fix and flip loans outside Texas?

Yes. ACP makes these loans in most other states, including Oklahoma, Illinois, Michigan, Indiana, Georgia and Virginia, on the same programs. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property, so send the address and ACP will match it to a program that lends there.

Can I refinance into a rental loan when the rehab is done?

Yes. Once the work is done you can refinance into a 30-year Texas DSCR loan sized on the finished value, with no 12-month wait, including cash-out.

How do I get a quote?

Send the property address, purchase price, repair budget, after-repair value, your middle credit score and how many flips you have finished in the last three years. ACP compares the deal across 90+ loan programs and comes back, usually the same day, with the advance, the cash to close and the program that fits.

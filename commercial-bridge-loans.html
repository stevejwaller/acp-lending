Commercial Bridge Loans

Short-term financing for commercial property that needs to close, reposition or get out of a maturing loan. ACP lends $500,000 to $5,000,000 on office, warehouse, retail, self-storage and light industrial property in Texas, Florida, Oklahoma, New Mexico and Arkansas. Underwriting is asset based: the property and your plan for it come first.

Terms

Loan size$500,000 to $5,000,000
Term12 to 36 months, interest only
PrepaymentNo prepayment penalty
LeverageUp to 65% LTV (35% down on purchases); improvements up to 65% of after-repair value
LienFirst lien only
Property typesOffice, warehouse, retail, self-storage and light industrial
UsesPurchase, refinance, cash-out, improvements, repositioning and payoff of a maturing loan
StatesTexas, Florida, Oklahoma, New Mexico and Arkansas

This program does not cover multifamily, 1–4 unit residential, raw land, ground-up construction or second liens. For those, see Multifamily Loans, Fix-and-Flip Bridge Loans and Ground-Up Construction Loans. Need long-term, 30-year fixed financing instead? See Commercial Real Estate Loans.

What to have ready

Credit authorization, recent bank statements, a personal financial statement, the property's rent roll or operating statement, and your exit plan: sale, lease-up or long-term refinance.

1. The Analysis

After-repair value

ARV comes only from closed sales inside the property’s own subdivision, matched on size and bedroom count. This sample used 22 closed sales over 14 months: renovated 3-bedroom homes from 1,350 to 1,650 sq ft sold at a median of $213 per sq ft, or $315,000 at 1,480 sq ft. Neighboring subdivisions are named and left out, not blended in. In your report the full comp list is attached as an exhibit (the HARMLS Agent Single Line).

Repairs

  • Kitchen and baths: $21,500
  • Flooring, paint and trim throughout: $14,000
  • Roof, HVAC service and water heater: $11,500
  • Exterior, landscaping and punch list: $8,000

Total $55,000, plus a 10% contingency carried in the profit and loss below. The line-item repair chart goes in the exhibits.

4. The Structure That Fits

Sample investor: 4 completed flips in the last 36 months, credit in the 680–719 range, $50,000 available, goal: highest advance, least cash out of pocket.

Best fit: 75% of ARV

Texas hard money, 12 months, interest-only. Cash due at closing $13,338. Liquidity needed: one-third of the repairs plus six months of interest, about $31,300, which this investor has.

Alternative: 70% of ARV

Same structure at the standard advance. Cash due at closing $28,615. More of the investor’s own money in the deal.

The Structure That Fits

Sample investor: 4 completed flips in the last 36 months, credit in the 680–719 range, $50,000 available, goal: highest advance, least cash out of pocket.

Texas: 70% of ARV

Texas hard money, 12 months, interest-only. Cash due at closing $28,615. Liquidity needed: one-third of the repairs plus six months of interest, about $30,500, which this investor has.

Outside Texas: 90/10

90% of the purchase price and 100% of the repairs, capped at 75% of ARV. Cash due at closing $27,645: the 10% down payment plus closing costs.

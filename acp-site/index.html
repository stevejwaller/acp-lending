Private Real Estate Lending for Investors

Real estate financing you can count on

Direct relationships with institutional capital partners allow ACP Lending to offer competitive rates and flexible terms across a full range of property investment strategies. Whether you’re fixing and flipping, building to rent, or buying income properties, we review every file and structure every deal.

What We Offer

  • Fix-and-Flip Bridge Loans: 90–100% financing, 12–24 month terms, 5-day close for investors flipping single-family and small multifamily properties nationwide.
  • DSCR Rental Loans: Long-term 30-year fixed financing on 1–4 unit rentals, qualifying on the property’s cash flow, not personal income. Flexible credit programs available.
  • Ground-Up Construction Loans: Construction-to-permanent financing from lot acquisition through stabilization for new residential and small commercial projects.
  • Multifamily Loans: 5+ unit properties with programs from $1M to $25M+ on primary and secondary markets.
  • Portfolio Loans: Full-reserve and no-reserve portfolio financing for investors with multiple properties and a history of successful exits.
  • Commercial Real Estate Loans: Land, office, retail, and other commercial property financing from $100K to $17.5M, 30-year fixed terms.

How We Work

Every deal is reviewed by Steve Waller. You get:

  • Direct contact with the decision-maker, not a loan officer
  • Fast underwriting and transparent closing costs
  • Flexible structures tailored to your exit strategy
  • Programs that fit both seasoned investors and first-timers
  • Relationships with institutional capital partners nationwide

Choose Your Financing

Each financing type serves a different strategy. Use the links below to see program details, terms, and eligibility:

  • Fix-and-Flip Bridge Loans — For wholesalers and fix-and-flip investors buying below market value
  • DSCR Rental Loans — For buy-and-hold investors converting bridges to permanent rental financing
  • Ground-Up Construction Loans — For development projects from lot through stabilization
  • Multifamily Loans — For 5+ unit income properties in strong markets
  • Portfolio Loans — For seasoned investors building a portfolio of hold properties
  • Commercial Real Estate Loans — For office, retail, land, and mixed-use commercial properties

Get Started

Three ways to explore your options:

  • Run a Free Deal Check — Get a 30–60 minute analysis of property value, repair costs, and available financing before you commit.
  • Use the DSCR Calculator — See what a rental property can support with long-term financing, based on rent and expenses.
  • Request Financing Information — Send property details and get a quote on rates and structure.

Bridge & Fix & Flip Loans

What is a fix-and-flip bridge loan?

Short-term financing for the purchase and renovation of an investment property, repaid when it sells or refinances. Up to 100% of purchase and 100% of renovation, tiered by credit and experience.

Frequently Asked Questions

How soon after completing the rehab can I refinance?
Typically 6 months of seasoning required for strong rental documentation. If property is leased and generating rent immediately post-rehab, refinance can occur faster with rent verification.

Does the property need to have a tenant before I refinance?
Not required, but preferred. Rent documentation strengthens terms. If vacant, we can use comparable market rent for valuation and qualification.

Can my matured bridge loan be refinanced into a long-term DSCR loan?
Yes. Bridge loans reaching their end date can extend into a DSCR 30-year fixed program or hold on rental terms, depending on property performance and your goals.

What if the rent doesn't quite cover the long-term payment?
The property funds from cashflow at the LTC/ARV cap. If rental income falls short, we can structure it on the collateral strength and your portfolio. Discuss with us before stabilization.

Can I take cash out when I refinance?
Cash-out refinance depends on LTV available and the property's income or value. Most refinances are rate-and-term, but cash-out is possible if equity allows.

Is there a prepayment penalty if I sell or pay off early?
Bridge loans typically have 1-2 year prepay windows. Long-term DSCR refinances have minimal or no prepay penalties, giving you flexibility to exit or sell when ready.

Ground-Up Construction Loans

What is a ground-up construction loan?

Financing for a build from land to completion, released in inspected draws, with interest only on funds drawn. $100,000 to $10 million at up to 85% of total project cost.

Terms

Loan amount$100,000 to $10 million
LeverageUp to 85% of total project cost and 70% of completed value
ExperienceNew builders considered; a completed-project track record unlocks higher leverage
Credit scoreAbout 650 minimum on some programs; others weigh the asset and builder more
PaymentsInterest-only, on funds drawn only
DrawsMilestone-based, verified by inspection
ValuationAppraisal or technology-based, by program
ProjectsSingle-family spec, infill, build-to-rent, 2–4 units
ExitBuild-to-sell or build-to-rent

Program maximums, subject to underwriting, appraisal or valuation, title and lender approval. Business-purpose lending only. Not a commitment to lend.

Texas Ground-Up Construction Loans

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 24, 2026

What are ACP’s Texas ground-up construction loan terms?

ACP Lending places ground-up construction loans on Texas spec homes, infill and build-to-rent projects from $100,000 to $10,000,000, at up to 85% of total project cost and 70% of completed value. Interest is charged only on money drawn, draws are paid after each stage is inspected, and rates run 9% to 12% depending on experience, liquidity and credit. First-time builders are considered with a strong contractor.

Texas construction loan terms at a glance

Loan size$100,000 to $10,000,000
LeverageUp to 85% of total project cost (land plus construction) and up to 70% of completed value; higher for builders with a track record
Rate9% to 12%, depending on experience, liquidity and credit
Points1% to 3% of the loan
Lender feesUnderwriting about $2,000; closing fee $500 to $1,000; plus title, attorney and other third-party costs
Credit score650 to 670 minimum, depending on program
PaymentsInterest-only, due monthly, on the drawn balance only
DrawsYou pay the contractor, submit the invoices, and draws are paid by ACH after the work is inspected
ProjectsSingle-family spec, infill, build-to-rent, and some 2–4 unit projects
ExperienceFirst-time builders considered; experience of the builder or general contractor counts

Texas construction loan questions

How much can I borrow to build a house in Texas as an investor?

Up to 85% of total project cost, land plus construction, and no more than 70% of the completed value. Builders with completed projects can go higher.

Do I pay interest on the whole loan during construction?

No. Interest is charged only on what has been drawn, and it’s due monthly. It isn’t rolled into the loan.

How do construction draws work?

You pay the contractor for a stage of work, submit the invoices, the work is inspected, and the draw is paid to you by ACH.

Can I get a construction loan with no building experience?

Yes, case by case. A licensed general contractor with completed projects, a detailed budget and permits in hand make the difference. Expect lower leverage than an experienced builder.

What credit score do I need?

Generally 650 to 670 at minimum. A stronger score, more liquidity and more completed projects lower the rate.

What do I need to send for a quote?

The lot address and what you paid or owe on it, plans, the construction budget and schedule, the builder, the expected completed value, your exit (sell or rent) and your middle credit score.

Most investors do not have the time to find the terms and the lender that match what they are trying to do. There are hundreds of lenders out there. When a deal is structured correctly, they compete for it. That is the job ACP does for you.

Who I Am

I have spent 35+ years in commercial finance, with senior roles at Comerica, JPMorgan Chase, U.S. Bank, Group 1 Automotive and Capital Automotive REIT, and I served in the U.S. Marine Corps. Over the last 18 months I have originated about 70 fix-and-flip, new construction and DSCR loans in Texas. ACP is based in Spring, Texas, and serves Houston and investors nationwide.

Your file is reviewed and structured by the person you talk to, from the first call to the closing table.

What ACP Does

1. Analyze the deal

Value from comparable sales in the same subdivision, the repair budget checked against the plan, and your cash due at closing worked out before you commit.

2. Structure it to get approved

Leverage, exit and documentation set up the way underwriters want to see them, so the file reads clean the first time.

3. Match it to the right program

Your deal is run against every program we work with and put in front of the lenders that fit, so they compete for your business.

How the Process Works

The ACP process in under a minute: the deal you send, the analysis, the match, what you receive and the results.

90 Lending Programs, Matched to You

ACP works from a matrix of 90 lending programs across 36 capital sources, covering fix-and-flip, DSCR rental, ground-up construction, multifamily, portfolio and commercial financing. Every file is run against all of them. Which one wins depends on what matters most to you on this deal:

Lowest rate

You want the cheapest money and are willing to pay reasonable fees to get it.

Highest advance

You want the most financing and the least cash out of pocket at closing.

Balanced

A reasonable rate and fees, with some of your own money in the deal.

About ACP Lending

Steve Waller, ACP Real Estate Lending. 30+ years in commercial banking and financial services. Based in Spring, Texas, serving Houston and investors nationwide.

Mission Statement

ACP Lending helps real estate investors evaluate a deal before they commit, then match it to the right financing. We underwrite the property and the plan so investors can move with confidence.

Mission

Provide clear, straightforward financing for real estate investors — from short-term fix-and-flip bridge loans to long-term DSCR rental, construction, multifamily, and commercial financing. Every deal is reviewed on the merits of the property and the plan.

Strategy

Match each deal to the best available program based on FICO, liquidity, and experience in the last 36 months. Offer both short-term and long-term options across residential, multifamily, and commercial properties. Deliver a Deal Book with value analysis, financing fit, and profitability so investors can decide with full information.

Goals

  • Help investors secure funding quickly for flips, ground-up construction, and portfolio growth
  • Provide transparent analysis so clients know their purchase points, sale points, and financing options
  • Build long-term relationships by underwriting the deal, not just the credit file
  • Serve Houston-area investors and clients nationwide with direct access to Steve Waller

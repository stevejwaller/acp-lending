Houston Hard Money Loans

Zero out of pocket fix-and-flip financing for Houston investors.

If the purchase, the repairs and the closing costs fit inside 70% of after-repair value, ACP funds all three. Rates from 9% to 12%, 1 to 2 points, and closing in 5 business days once a complete file is received.

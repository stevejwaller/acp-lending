Three Fix-and-Flip Programs

Zero Out of Pocket

Up to 70% of after-repair value. When the purchase, the repairs and the closing costs all fit inside 70% of ARV, nothing is due at closing. Sized on the collateral, your experience and a lookback on credit history, not a credit score.

90 / 100

90% of the purchase price and 100% of the renovation budget. You bring the closing costs. Lower rate and fees than the zero out of pocket program. Qualification is set by credit score and experience.

100 / 100

100% of the purchase price and 100% of the renovation budget. You bring the closing costs. Lower rate and fees than the zero out of pocket program. For borrowers with a 720 credit score and 10 deals closed in the last 36 months.

Real Estate Investor Loan FAQ

What types of real estate investor financing does ACP Lending offer?

ACP Lending originates and underwrites bridge and fix-and-flip, DSCR rental, ground-up construction, multifamily and portfolio financing directly with its institutional capital partners.

How does DSCR rental financing work?

DSCR financing is evaluated primarily on a rental property’s cash flow rather than the borrower’s personal income. Approval, terms and required coverage ratios vary by lender and property.

How quickly can investment-property financing close?

Fix-and-flip financing closes in 5 business days once a complete file is received. Timing on DSCR, construction, multifamily and portfolio financing depends on the program, appraisal, title work and a complete borrower file.

Where is financing available?

ACP Lending offers real estate investor financing across most U.S. markets through direct relationships with institutional capital partners. Program availability varies by property and location.

What information is needed for a property analysis?

Start with the property address, purchase price or current value, estimated renovation budget, requested loan amount, expected rent or resale value, and your investment experience.

Long-Term Financing (30-Year Terms)

Permanent financing for rental properties, DSCR loans, and construction projects. Build equity and cash flow with fixed-rate options.

DSCR LoansDSCR

  • Program: 30-yr fixed, ARM, or Interest-Only
  • Loan Size: $85K–$2M+
  • Min FICO: 640 (no minimum on some programs)
  • LTV: 80% purchase / 75% cash-out
  • Min DSCR: 0.75 (or no minimum for higher rate)
  • Properties: 1–10+ units, STR allowed
  • Docs: No income verification options
  • Note: Foreign nationals welcome

Ground-Up ConstructionCONST

  • Program: Construction + Permanent financing
  • Loan Size: $100K–$2M+
  • Min FICO: 670
  • LTC: 90% total costs (some 75% ARV)
  • Draw Period: 12–24 months
  • Interest: Monthly on drawn balance
  • Rate: 9–12%
  • Note: Borrower or GC partner pays contractors

Multifamily (5+ Units)MF

  • Program: DSCR rental + Multifamily Bridge
  • Loan Size: $250K–$10M
  • Min FICO: 700
  • LTV: 65–75% depending on structure
  • Units: 5–30+ units
  • Min DSCR: 1.15–1.25
  • Terms: 15–30 year amortization
  • Note: Portfolio option available

Portfolio Loans (SFR Rentals)PORT

  • Program: Hold 3+ rental properties in one loan
  • Loan Size: $250K–$3M
  • Min FICO: 680
  • LTV: 80% / 75% cash-out
  • Properties: 3–20 SFR units
  • Min DSCR: 1.0 (portfolio average)
  • Rate: Based on combined DSCR
  • Note: Consolidate holdings into one loan

Commercial Real EstateCRE

  • Program: Retail, Office, Industrial, Mixed-Use
  • Loan Size: $200K–$17.5M
  • Min FICO: 600
  • LTV: 65–75% based on property type
  • Min DSCR: 1.15–1.25
  • Terms: 5–20 year amortization
  • Investor: Investor or owner-user conventional, SBA 504
  • Note: All states, no banked financing

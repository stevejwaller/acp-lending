ACP Lending Financing Options

Choose your financing strategy: Short-term bridge loans or long-term permanent financing

Short-Term Bridge Loans (6–24 Months)

Bridge financing for acquisitions, renovations, and repositioning. Quick funding for fix-and-flip, fix-and-rent, stabilized bridge, and new construction. Available for single-family and multifamily properties.

Fix & FlipFLIP

  • Program: 90–100% Purchase / 100% Rehab
  • Loan Size: $75K–$2M
  • Min FICO: 640–720 (by lender)
  • Closing: 5–48 hours
  • Rate: 9–12%
  • Points: 1–2%
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily
  • Term: 6–24 months

Fix & RentFIXRENT

  • Program: 90–100% Purchase / 100% Rehab
  • Loan Size: $75K–$2M
  • Min FICO: 640–720 (by lender)
  • Closing: 7–10 days
  • Rate: 9–12%
  • Exit: Rent out or refinance to DSCR
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily
  • Term: 12–18 months

Stabilized BridgeBRIDGE

  • Program: Loan on stabilized property pending refinance
  • Loan Size: $75K–$3M
  • Min FICO: 640–700 (by lender)
  • LTV: 70–75% of property value
  • Rate: 10–13%
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily
  • Exit: Refinance to DSCR or permanent financing
  • Term: 6–24 months

ConstructionCONST

  • Program: Short-term construction financing
  • Loan Size: $100K–$2M
  • Min FICO: 670
  • LTC: 90% of total project costs
  • Draw Period: 12–24 months
  • Interest: Monthly on drawn balance
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily new builds
  • Exit: Refinance to permanent financing

Long-Term Permanent Financing (5–30 Year Terms)

Take-out and permanent financing for stabilized properties. Build equity with fixed-rate options, DSCR loans, cash-out refinances, and commercial property financing for single-family and multifamily properties.

Rate & Term RefinanceRATE-TERM

  • Program: Refinance existing loan at better rate
  • Loan Size: $85K–$2M+
  • Min FICO: 640–700
  • LTV: 80% (or higher by property value)
  • Closing: 7–14 days
  • Terms: 15–30 year fixed
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily rentals
  • No income docs required

DSCR (Buy & Hold)DSCR

  • Program: 30-yr fixed, ARM, or Interest-Only
  • Loan Size: $85K–$2M+
  • Min FICO: 640 (or no minimum for higher rate)
  • LTV: 80% purchase / 75% cash-out
  • Min DSCR: 0.75 and up
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily rentals
  • Docs: No income verification required
  • Note: Foreign nationals welcome

Cash-Out RefinanceCASHOUT

  • Program: Refinance and pull equity from property
  • Loan Size: $85K–$2M+
  • Min FICO: 640–700
  • LTV: 75% cash-out (80% purchase)
  • Min DSCR: 0.75 and up
  • Use of Funds: Property improvements, debt payoff, capital
  • Properties: Single-family & multifamily rentals
  • Terms: 15–30 year fixed

Commercial PropertiesCRE

  • Property Types: Retail, Office, Industrial, Mixed-Use
  • Loan Size: $200K–$17.5M
  • Min FICO: 600
  • LTV: 65–75% based on type
  • Min DSCR: 1.15–1.25
  • Terms: 5–20 year amortization
  • Investor or Owner-User: Both conventional and SBA 504
  • Units: Any size multifamily

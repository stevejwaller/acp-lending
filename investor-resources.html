How These Deals Get Underwritten

The financing is only one part of a successful investment. These resources explain how ACP Real Estate Lending evaluates value, leverage, borrower contribution, rental cash flow, and project profitability so investors can make better decisions before committing capital.

How is preliminary ARV evaluated?

A credible after-repair value should be supported by comparable properties that compete with the subject in location, size, age, condition, design, and market appeal. Nearby sales are not automatically valid comps. Major roads, subdivision boundaries, school zones, new construction, unusual lots, and significant differences in size or architecture can materially affect value. Active and pending listings also matter because they show the competition the renovated property may face.

How does the 70% all-in structure work?

Under a 70% all-in program, the maximum total loan is generally calculated as 70% of the supported ARV. Renovation funds, lender fees, and applicable closing costs are allocated first; the remaining amount is available toward the purchase. A deal can potentially produce little or no cash required at closing only when the purchase price and all other funded costs fit within the maximum loan. Liquidity and reserve requirements still apply.

How does a DSCR rental loan qualify?

Debt-service-coverage-ratio financing focuses primarily on whether the property’s qualifying rent supports the proposed housing expense. Taxes, insurance, association dues, interest rate, loan amount, and amortization all affect DSCR. A property with attractive gross rent can still fail to qualify if expenses or the proposed debt payment are too high.

What information produces a useful quote?

For purchase or renovation financing, send the property address, contract or purchase price, renovation budget, estimated ARV, intended exit strategy, estimated credit score, experience, liquidity, and target closing date. For rental financing, also include current or projected rent, taxes, insurance, association dues, and requested loan amount. Complete information produces a more reliable preliminary structure.

Fix-and-Flip Bridge Loans

Terms: Fix-and-Flip Bridge Loans

What is fix-and-flip bridge financing?

Fix-and-flip bridge financing is short-term capital for real estate investors purchasing and rehabbing single-family or small multifamily investment properties. ACP Lending structures these as interest-only financing with 12–24 month terms, available to individual or LLC borrowers.

How much financing can I get for a fix-and-flip deal?

ACP Lending offers up to 100% purchase financing and up to 100% renovation financing. The purchase advance is tiered by completed project history, starting at 90% and moving to 95% and then 100%, and both are capped by the lower of loan-to-cost and 70% to 75% of after-repair value, so whichever of those comes in tighter is what sizes the deal.

For Texas properties, a 70% advance structure is also available. When the purchase price, renovation budget, and closing costs all fit within that 70% of after-repair value, the deal may close with nothing out of pocket.

All figures are subject to underwriting, appraisal or valuation, and lender approval.

How fast can fix-and-flip financing close with ACP Lending?

Fix-and-flip financing closes in 5 business days once a complete file is received. Exact timelines depend on appraisal or valuation, title, insurance, and documentation turnaround.

What information do I need to submit to get a loan review?

ACP Lending asks for the property address, purchase price, estimated repairs, after-repair value (ARV), intended exit strategy, and an estimated FICO score to provide a preliminary loan review.

Can I borrow through an LLC for fix-and-flip financing?

Yes. ACP Lending's fix-and-flip bridge financing is available to both individual and LLC borrowers.

DSCR Rental Loans

Terms: DSCR Rental Loans

What is the minimum DSCR required?

Requirements vary by lending program. Some programs allow a DSCR as low as 0.80×, while others require a minimum of 1.00×. The available option depends on the specific program and the overall strength of the file.

What credit score do I need for DSCR financing?

Programs are available with credit scores as low as approximately 640, though many programs require 660 or higher to access the best leverage and pricing.

What's the maximum LTV on DSCR financing?

Up to 80% on purchase and rate-and-term refinance. Cash-out refinances are typically capped lower, generally around 75%.

Is there a prepayment penalty?

It depends on the program. Some DSCR programs carry no prepayment penalty after the third year, while others use an adjustable prepayment structure of up to five years.

What loan amounts are available?

DSCR financing generally starts around $70,000, with higher loan amounts considered case by case depending on the program and the property.

Ground-Up Construction Loans

Terms: Ground-Up Construction Loans

What loan amounts are available for ground-up construction?

Generally from $100,000 up to $10 million, depending on the program and the scope of the project.

How is construction financing disbursed?

Funds are released through milestone-based draws as work is completed and verified by inspection, rather than in one lump sum. Interest is charged only on funds actually drawn.

Does my experience level affect the leverage I can get?

On some programs, yes. Borrowers with a track record of completed projects can access materially higher leverage than first-time builders, though new investors may still be considered.

Is an appraisal required?

It depends on the program. Some require a standard appraisal-based valuation; others use a technology-based valuation approach instead of a traditional appraisal.

What credit score is needed?

Requirements vary by program—some require a minimum credit score around 650, while others focus more heavily on the asset and the builder’s experience than on traditional credit and income documentation.

Multifamily Loans

Terms: Multifamily Loans

What loan amounts are available for multifamily properties?

Short-term bridge financing ranges from $250,000 to $2,000,000. Long-term financing for 5–9 unit properties ranges from $150,000 to $1,500,000.

What's the difference between the short-term and long-term programs?

The short-term bridge program (12–18 months, with extensions available) is designed for acquisition, renovation, or value-add repositioning of 5+ unit properties. The long-term program (30-year term) is for stabilized 5–9 unit properties and requires a higher minimum credit score.

What LTV is available on multifamily financing?

Short-term bridge financing offers up to 75% LTV on purchase, 70% on refinance, and 65% on cash-out refinance. The long-term program offers up to 70% LTV on purchase and refinance, and 65% on cash-out.

Is renovation or rehab financing available?

Yes. A value-add variant of the short-term bridge program offers up to 80% of the purchase price plus 100% of rehab costs for experienced borrowers.

What credit score is required?

The short-term bridge program requires a minimum credit score of approximately 650; the long-term program requires approximately 700.

Portfolio Loans

Terms: Portfolio Loans

What's the minimum portfolio size?

Portfolio financing is typically structured for 20 to 100+ units, with scattered-site and multi-state portfolios accepted.

What loan amounts are available?

Portfolio facilities generally range from $2 million to $75 million or more, depending on the size and composition of the portfolio.

What LTV is available?

Up to 70%, tiered by credit score—borrowers with a 720+ score can access up to 70% on purchase, while scores in the 680–699 range are typically capped closer to 60%. Refinance and cash-out are capped somewhat lower.

Do properties need to be leased already?

Yes, portfolios generally need to be at least 90% leased to qualify.

Is this a recourse loan?

No. Portfolio facilities are typically structured on a non-recourse basis, with flexible prepayment options.

Commercial Real Estate Loans

Terms: Commercial Real Estate Loans

What property types do you finance?

Office, retail, warehouse and industrial, self-storage, automotive and auto service, mixed-use, multifamily of 5 or more units, hospitality, assisted living and other special-purpose property.

What loan amounts are available?

Commercial financing generally runs from $100,000 to $17.5 million, depending on the property, the borrower and the program.

How much leverage can I get?

Investor purchases and refinances generally run 65% to 75% of value. Owner-users occupying at least 51% of the building can reach up to 90% through the SBA 504 program.

What credit score do I need?

Programs reach credit scores as low as 600, and a lower-leverage option is available for borrowers with recent credit events or no score at all.

Is there a balloon payment?

No. Long-term commercial financing is a 30-year fully amortizing loan with no balloon. A 24-month interest-only bridge is available for properties that need time to lease up or stabilize.

Houston Hard Money Loans

Terms: Houston Hard Money Loans

Does ACP Lending finance fix-and-flip deals in Houston?

Yes. ACP Lending is based in Spring, Texas and finances fix-and-flip and bridge projects across Harris, Fort Bend, Montgomery, Brazoria, Galveston and Chambers counties, with individual and LLC borrowers.

Can I close a Houston flip with nothing out of pocket?

On Texas properties, when the purchase price, the renovation budget and the closing costs all fit inside 70% of after-repair value, ACP can fund all three and nothing is due at closing. The Deal Estimator shows your cash due at closing before you apply.

What are the rates and points?

Fix-and-flip rates run 9% to 12% interest-only, with 1 to 2 points. The 90/100 and 100/100 programs carry a lower rate and lower fees than the zero out of pocket program. Pricing depends on program, leverage, credit and experience.

How fast can a Houston fix-and-flip loan close?

Fix-and-flip financing closes in 5 business days once a complete file is received.

Is there a minimum loan amount or credit score?

The minimum loan is $75,000. ACP runs a soft credit pull, which puts no inquiry on your report. The 100/100 program requires a 720 credit score and 10 deals closed in the last 36 months; the zero out of pocket program is sized on the collateral, experience and a lookback on credit history rather than a score.

Commercial Real Estate Loans

How does ACP finance commercial real estate?

ACP Lending structures long-term, 30-year fixed financing with no balloon for commercial investment properties, including office, retail, warehouse, self-storage, automotive, assisted living homes, 5+ unit multifamily and mixed-use properties, with no minimum credit score on certain programs.

Assisted Living Homes (1–4 Units)

  • 30-year fixed, no balloon, with a 10-year interest-only option
  • Purchase and cash-out refinance
  • Up to 75% LTV on purchases (70% for first-time investors), $75,000 to $2,000,000, with no minimum credit score
  • Up to $10,000,000 at 65% LTV, 600+ credit, for experienced investors in major metros
  • No major structural renovations

Long-Term (5+ Units and Mixed-Use)

  • 30-year fixed, no balloon, from $100,000
  • Up to 75% LTV on purchases (70% for first-time investors)
  • No minimum credit score

What We Review

Property location, value, use, rent roll or operating income, occupancy, borrower liquidity, experience, and exit strategy. Every transaction is evaluated individually. Manufactured homes, land and hospitality are not eligible on the 30-year fixed programs.

Commercial Loan FAQ

What commercial properties does ACP finance?

Investment commercial real estate including office, retail, warehouse, self-storage and automotive properties, assisted living homes (1–4 units), 5+ unit multifamily and mixed-use properties, with 30-year fixed financing and no balloon.

Is there a minimum credit score?

No minimum credit score on certain programs, with a 50% LTV option for recent credit events.

Can I refinance out of a bridge loan into a commercial long-term loan?

Yes. Refinancing out of a bridge loan into 30-year fixed financing is available on 5+ unit, mixed-use and commercial properties.

Rental Loans When DSCR Doesn’t Work

What if my rental doesn’t qualify for DSCR?

When the rent doesn’t cover the full payment, your income can qualify the loan instead. ACP offers two 30-year options: Full-Doc Conventional, qualified on W-2s or tax returns, and Bank-Statement, qualified on 12–24 months of deposits instead of tax returns.

Full-Doc Conventional

Qualifies onW-2s and pay stubs and/or 2 years of tax returns; rent counted from Schedule E or 75% of the lease
Structure30-year fixed, no balloon, no prepayment penalty
Down paymentAbout 15% on a single-family rental; about 25% on 2–4 units
RateUsually the lowest of the long-term rental options
VestingPersonal name
Property countUp to 10 financed properties
ReservesSeveral months of payments per financed property

Advantages

  • Usually the lowest rate — a smaller payment and better monthly cash flow
  • 30-year fixed with no prepayment penalty — sell or refinance anytime
  • Lower down payment, and the rent counts toward your income
  • Standard guidelines, so terms are predictable and fees are typically lower

Bank-Statement

Qualifies onAverage deposits from 12–24 months of personal or business bank statements (business accounts use an expense factor)
Tax returnsNot required
Structure30-year terms; interest-only available on some programs
Down paymentAbout 20–25%
RateAbove conventional, often close to DSCR pricing
VestingPersonal name; LLC allowed on some programs
Property countNo agency cap
PrepaymentCommon on investment-property programs

Advantages

  • No tax returns — keep every legitimate write-off and still qualify
  • Qualifies on your real cash flow, not your taxable income
  • No 10-property cap, so you can keep growing the portfolio
  • Covers the gap when the property won’t DSCR and the returns won’t qualify conventional

Which one fits?

Rent covers the full paymentDSCR — no income documents, LLC OK
Strong W-2s or tax returns, fewer than 10 financed propertiesFull-Doc Conventional
Self-employed with strong, steady depositsBank-Statement

Figures are typical ranges and vary by program, credit, property and loan size. Not a commitment to lend.

Rental Loan FAQ

Why didn’t my rental qualify for DSCR?

DSCR is monthly rent divided by the full payment: principal, interest, taxes, insurance and HOA. In Texas, taxes and insurance often push the payment above the rent. A $300,000 house renting for $2,200 with a $2,500 payment comes out at 0.88, and most DSCR programs want 1.0 or better.

Is a bank-statement loan a conventional loan?

No. Conventional loans follow Fannie Mae and Freddie Mac guidelines and require full income documentation. Bank-statement loans are non-QM programs that qualify you on 12 to 24 months of deposits instead of tax returns.

Can I close a conventional rental loan in my LLC?

Generally no. Conventional investment-property loans close in your personal name. Some bank-statement programs allow an LLC, and DSCR programs generally do.

How many rentals can I finance with conventional loans?

Conventional guidelines cap you at 10 financed properties. Bank-statement and DSCR programs have no agency cap.

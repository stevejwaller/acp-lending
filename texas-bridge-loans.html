Texas Hard Money and Bridge Loans for Investors

By Steve Waller · Terms updated November 25, 2026

What is a bridge loan for a Texas real estate investor?

A short-term, interest-only loan, often called a hard money loan, that carries an investment property until it is sold or refinanced into long-term debt. ACP Lending makes bridge loans in Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and the rest of Texas, from $50,000, for purchases, renovations, and refinances of hard money loans that have matured or are about to. Terms run 6 to 24 months, rates start at 9.99%, and the loan is sized on the property’s value and your exit. ACP makes the same loans in most other states.

Texas bridge loan questions

My hard money loan is maturing and the house isn’t done. What can I do?

Refinance into a new bridge loan to finish the work, or into a 30-year DSCR loan if the property is rentable now. Send the payoff letter, the remaining scope of work and the current value. If you’ve kept making the monthly payments, a matured loan is treated as current.

Can I refinance out of a bridge loan into a long-term rental loan?

Yes. Once the property is finished, a 30-year DSCR loan can pay off the bridge, sized on the finished appraised value. No 12-month wait, including cash-out, and no tenant needed on 1–4 units.

How much can I borrow on a bridge refinance?

It depends on the property’s current value, your credit, your experience and the program. Any remaining renovation budget can be held back for draws. Send the payoff letter and the value, and ACP comes back with a number, usually the same day.

Do you make hard money and bridge loans in Houston and Dallas?

Yes. ACP makes hard money and bridge loans statewide, including Houston, Dallas–Fort Worth, San Antonio, Austin and their suburbs.

Do you make bridge loans outside Texas?

Yes. ACP makes these loans in most other states, including Oklahoma, Illinois, Michigan, Indiana, Georgia and Virginia, on the same programs. Some programs exclude a few states and rural property, so send the address and ACP will match it to a program that lends there.

Is a bridge loan the same as a hard money loan?

Close to it. Both are short-term, interest-only loans sized mainly on the property. “Hard money” usually means a private lender with higher rates; bridge programs from institutional lenders often cost less.

How do I get a quote?

Send the property address, what you owe and when it matures (or the purchase price), the repairs left, the value, and your middle credit score. ACP compares the deal across 90+ loan programs and comes back, usually the same day.

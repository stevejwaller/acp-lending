Ask a Lender

Short answers to the questions investors ask us most, each about 30 seconds. Have a question of your own? Ask a lender and we’ll answer it.

How fast can you close?

How quickly a quote comes back, how long closing takes once your file is complete, and when an appraisal can be skipped.

What credit score do I need?

The credit score our fix-and-flip and rental programs start at, and what a higher score gets you.

Can I get a loan on my first flip?

Yes, several of our programs take first-time flippers. What to expect on loan size, and when a heavy rehab needs experience.

How much will you lend on a fix and flip?

We size most fix-and-flip loans at the lower of 90% of your total cost and 75% of the after-repair value.

What is a DSCR loan?

A DSCR loan qualifies a rental on the property’s rent instead of your personal income. Here’s how the ratio works.

What should I send for a quote?

The numbers to send for a fix-and-flip quote or a rental quote, so we can come back with real terms.

Do you only lend in Texas?

We focus on Houston, Dallas-Fort Worth, San Antonio and Austin, and our programs are available nationwide.

What won't you finance?

We make business-purpose loans on investment property only. What falls outside that, and our $50,000 minimum loan.

What do I need to have ready when I ask for a loan?

Three things to have ready before you ask for a loan: your credit score, your liquidity and your experience.

What's the difference between Dutch and non-Dutch interest on a bridge or fix-and-flip loan?

Dutch vs. non-Dutch interest is whether you pay interest on the rehab money before you draw it.

Should I finance my loan payments into the loan (an interest reserve)?

An interest reserve lets you skip monthly payments during the rehab. The upside, the cost and the risk.

Case Study: A Wholesale Deal With No Price, and Why That Helped

Same House, Two Buyers: Why the Repair Scope Decides the Deal

November 1, 2026

One buyer scoped the repairs line by line; the other planned from photos and ran out of cash when the roof, A/C and foundation surfaced. ACP sizes repairs at what a third-party contractor would charge, and any repair funds you don’t use come back to you when the loan pays off.
